    What the listing check covers

    You paste the address of a page that lists you, and we read that page and tell you whether it shows your details. We do not search broker sites for you. A page on any site can be read. We recognise nine by name: seven in the United States (Spokeo, Whitepages, BeenVerified, TruthFinder, FastPeopleSearch, MyLife, Intelius), White Pages Australia, and Truecaller, which we cannot read because it needs a sign-in, so we give you its opt-out guide instead.

    “Not listed” means that page did not show you. It is not clearance: you can be listed on pages you did not paste, in any country.

    When you press Scan me, MyPrivacyTOOL does what trackers do, in the open. We look up the details you give us (email, name, phone, usernames) in outside services such as breach databases and public sites. We record your device and network details, including your IP address. Then we show you what each one found, who saw what, and how to stop it. If you sign up, we keep a record so we can keep watching for you. If you don't, we delete your personal record 7 days after your report is delivered, and you can delete it sooner, free, any time. Pressing Scan me means you agree.
