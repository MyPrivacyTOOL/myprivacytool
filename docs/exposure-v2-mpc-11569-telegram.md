**The blocker is not the evidence, it is that no person receives this scan.** The baseline scan runs only for allow-listed test accounts (`PROBE_TELEGRAM_IDS`); `TELEGRAM_SCAN_SCOPE` is not set, and `docs/telegram-rollout-gates.md` lists the open gates before real users (legal pass on the wording, a deletion test on a real account, `PROBE_MODE` confirmation, language plan, paid-plan copy). `tileState.js` defines Live as running today, and the /footprint/ note says "one of the checks MPT runs today". Marking Telegram Live now would tell visitors they can get a scan they cannot get. So all 30 stay **In build**, and the 14 are ready to move the day real-user scope opens (a one-line change in `tileState.js`, with the 11 following once a probe returns them). No check needs a forwarded message: the forward-dependent Telegram ids are the 8 extras (outside the 30, MPC-11568).

Single-snapshot caveat: the evidence is one scan on one test account three weeks ago. It shows the code path works end to end; it says nothing about how often fields return for real people.

## What a Telegram score means (decisions for the owner)
1. **Weight of the `telegram` category: 10, PROPOSED, not approved** (same as email). It only matters when a scan also has web or email results; on a Telegram-only scan the one active category carries the whole score.
2. **`not_returned` maps to `clear`.** It means "this check ran and the bot received nothing", never "hidden" or "private" (strategy 11.4). The alternative is `not_checked`, which would make every Telegram score either 100 or empty.
3. **Severity is each hexagon's approved catalogue risk tier** (2026-10-28): 6 low, 11 medium, 8 high among the 25 (total 52). Today's Telegram number, risk-count-1 (owner decision 2026-10-26), counts every returned hexagon as 1 of 30 with no weights, and the bot's reply still says that. Scoring Telegram under v2 weights it instead. The owner test snapshot reads **14 of 30 = 47% (medium) today and 62 (medium) under v2**; the worst account reads 83% today (the five never-evaluated sit in the denominator) and 100 under v2.
4. **85, not 91** (above).
5. **Wiring is a separate gated step.** Nothing calls `computeExposureV2All`. If the Telegram worker is to show or record a v2 score, that is a new step needing sign-off, the real-user gates, and `TELEGRAM_*` switches, all held.
