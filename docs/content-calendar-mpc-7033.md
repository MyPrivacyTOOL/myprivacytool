# MPC-7033 content calendar (13 weeks from 2026-10-07)

`content-calendar-mpc-7033.csv` is generated by `scripts/generate-content-calendar.mjs`
(`node scripts/generate-content-calendar.mjs > docs/content-calendar-mpc-7033.csv`).

- 156 pieces: 39 blog (Thu/Sat/Tue), 91 X (daily), 26 Reddit (Fri/Mon, r/privacy + r/cybersecurity).
- Every link carries `utm_source` (blog/x/reddit), `utm_medium`, and a weekly `utm_campaign` (`mpc-7033-wNN`) for GA4.
- Week 1 blog leads with the existing pillar post `/blog/how-exposed-are-you`.

## Caveats
- Only the pillar post exists in `blogPosts.json`. The other blog slugs are planned posts; they must be written and
  added before their dates, otherwise the links 404. Titles are working titles.
- X and Reddit rows are topic prompts, not final copy.
- 13 full weeks from Wed 2026-10-07 end Tue 2027-01-05; the spec's 156-piece count was kept rather than truncating at Dec 31.
- Publishing is not done here. LinkedIn: see `channels/linkedin-publishing.md` (MPC-7504). X/Reddit posting is blocked on the MPC-6594 decision (Make.com broadcaster vs manual)
  and on confirming X/Reddit account access.
