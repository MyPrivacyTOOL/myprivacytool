# Release notes: exposure score method v2 (MPC-11569)

**We now check more. You did not become more exposed.**

Your score now counts more checks, so it can be higher or lower than before. That reflects what we look at, not a change in what you reveal. Scores from different methods cannot be compared with each other.

What is new
- When you give us an email address, your report is scored on **60 checks**: the 54 browser checks plus 6 email checks. Method name: `exposure-v2`.
- The six email checks that count: name in your address, mail provider and domain, public profile picture (Gravatar), second profile-picture service (Libravatar), website ownership record (RDAP), and public code changes (GitHub). Their badges on the footprint page are already Live (PR 942).
- Known breaches is not built yet, so it is not counted and stays In build.

What you may notice
- Your score can be higher or lower than on an earlier report.
- Coverage is now out of 60, so the same browser checks cover a smaller share than before.
- A scan with no email result scores exactly as it did.

What did not change
- The 54 browser checks, the scan counts, the bands (0-39 Low, 40-69 Medium, 70-100 High) and exposure-v1 are unchanged. The footprint catalogue still lists 650 hexagons.

Where it appears: the JSON attachment of the report (`exposure_v2`, with this sentence), and the method page `/how-we-calculate-your-score`. The report email body has no score line.
