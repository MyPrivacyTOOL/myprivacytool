# Release notes: exposure score method v2 (MPC-11569)

**We now check more things than before, so your score can change. You did not become more exposed.**

What is new
- When you give us an email address, your report is scored on **61 checks**: the 54 browser checks plus 7 email checks. Method name: `exposure-v2`.
- Six of the email checks run today and now count: name in your address, mail provider and domain, public profile picture (Gravatar), second profile-picture service (Libravatar), website ownership record (RDAP), and public code changes (GitHub). Their badges on the footprint page change from In build to Live.
- The seventh, known breaches, is not available yet. It counts in the 61 but is always shown as not checked.

What you may notice
- Your score can be higher or lower than on an earlier report. The new checks add information; they do not change what is true about you.
- Coverage is now out of 61, so the same browser checks cover a smaller share than before.
- A scan with no email result scores exactly as it did.

What did not change
- The 54 browser checks, the scan counts, the bands (0-39 Low, 40-69 Medium, 70-100 High) and exposure-v1 are unchanged. The footprint catalogue still lists 650 hexagons; only badges moved (Live 54 to 60).
- Scores from different versions should not be compared as improvement or decline.
